For grown-ups: learning content, privacy & progress

This starter contains 50 source-documented words and phrases. Community speaker review is pending. Roman spelling, pronunciation and respectful forms can vary. Ask a fluent speaker to check forms before teaching them as your family’s preferred usage.

Pronunciation practice means listening together. Hatti Quest does not automatically score accents or invent Badaga speech. The guide uses authored tips and source-linked words; an optional configured model may select existing content.

Progress is saved locally without an account. Export a progress file to keep a backup or move it to another browser. Audio is separate and is not included in this export.

BUILT FROM BELONGING

Our language.
Their little everyday moments.

A note from Siva Babu, creator of Hatti Quest.

“I’m Siva Babu, and I’m Badaga.”

The Badaga community is rooted in the Nilgiri Hills of Tamil Nadu, India. Our language carries everyday conversations, family memories and a sense of belonging.

For children growing up outside India, chances to hear and speak Badaga can be few and far between. A child may love their family and their roots, yet struggle to find the words in a conversation with a grandparent.

I’m building Hatti Quest to help bring our language into those small, ordinary moments. A playful adventure. A word heard in a familiar voice. A conversation that continues after the screen is put away.

AI can help us find and organise learning material. Families and fluent speakers give that material its meaning and its voice. My hope is that children feel curious, connected and comfortable trying—one little word at a time.

h.
