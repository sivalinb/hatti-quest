THE CHALLENGE OF KEEPING A LANGUAGE PRESENT

An oral tradition.
Many ways to write.

Badaga has no single broadly standardised native orthography.
Roman, Tamil and Kannada writing are used; dedicated scripts have been proposed.

01

Keep the sound

A written form needs the context and voice of a fluent speaker.

02

Respect variation

Families can have different spellings, forms and everyday usage.

03

Keep it alive

Language preservation includes real chances to hear and speak it.

Sources: community script discussions; Paul Hockings’ published correspondence, 2013.
